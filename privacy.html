Clockdown Privacy Policy

Effective 5 November 2026 · App: Clockdown (com.rishabh.clockdown) · Developer: ELOCENT

In short: your Amizone login, timetable, attendance and LMS data stay on your phone. Clockdown has no account of its own, shows no ads, and never sells or shares your data. The only things that leave your phone for the developer are optional crash reports and an optional anonymous version count, and you can switch both off in Settings.

Who we are

Clockdown is made by ELOCENT, Fazalwas Residential House, Manesar, Panchgaon, Gurugram, Haryana 122413, India. Contact: support@elocent.com.

Clockdown is an independent app. It is not made by, endorsed by or affiliated with Amity University, Amizone or Amity's LMS. It signs in to those services on your behalf, using your own login, and only to show you your own data.

Your Amizone ID and password

  • You sign in on Amizone's own sign-in page, shown inside the app.
  • After a successful sign-in, your ID and password are saved on your phone only, encrypted (AES-256, with a key held in Android's Keystore), so the sign-in page can be filled in for you next time.
  • They are sent only to Amizone (s.amizone.net), never to the developer or anyone else.
  • You can delete them at any time with Settings → Forget saved login, or by uninstalling the app.

Data Clockdown reads for you

Using your sign-in, Clockdown reads from Amizone and from Amity's LMS (alms06.amizone.net):

  • your timetable, attendance and holiday calendar;
  • your LMS courses, assignments, deadlines, announcements, course files and course leaderboards.

This data is stored only on your phone, to show it in the app and its widgets and to set your alarms and reminders. It is not sent to the developer or to anyone else.

Camera and files

If you use the page scanner to submit an assignment, the camera is used to photograph pages, which are turned into a PDF on your phone. The PDF only leaves your phone if you upload it to the LMS yourself. Course files you open are downloaded from the LMS and kept on your phone.

Crash reports (optional)

If the app crashes, or can't read a page it needs, it can send a short report to Sentry, the crash-reporting service the developer uses. A report contains the error and where in the app it happened, the app version, your phone model, Android version and system WebView version, and in some cases whether you were on Wi-Fi or mobile data. It never contains your Amizone ID or password, cookies, your name, your timetable, attendance or course content. Turn it off in Settings → Crash reports.

Anonymous version count (optional)

At most once a day, the app sends a random number it made up for this install, the app version and whether the app has been updated since it was installed, to a Google Sheet run by the developer, to know how many people use which version. The number is not tied to you, your phone or your Amizone account. Turn it off in Settings → Count this install.

Other services the app talks to

  • Google Play, to install and update the app (covered by Google's privacy policy).
  • YouTube and other websites, only when you open a video or link your teacher added to a course.
  • Copies of the app downloaded from GitHub (outside Google Play) also check GitHub for new versions.

What we don't do

  • No ads and no advertising or analytics trackers.
  • No selling or sharing of your data with anyone.
  • No location, contacts, microphone or call-log access.

Keeping and deleting your data

Everything Clockdown stores is on your phone. Signing out of Amizone in Settings removes your session and the Amizone and LMS data the app read; "Forget saved login" removes your saved ID and password; uninstalling the app removes everything. Crash reports are kept by Sentry for up to 90 days. The version count holds no personal data. To ask about or remove anything the developer holds, email support@elocent.com.

Security

All connections use HTTPS. Your saved login is encrypted on the phone, and the app's sign-in pages only load Amity's own sites and Cloudflare's captcha.

Who the app is for

Clockdown is meant for university students aged 18 and over. It is not directed at children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
